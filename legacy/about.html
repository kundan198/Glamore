How It Started

Born from a Love of Beauty

Glamore was founded in 2018 by renowned stylist Aria Fontaine, who envisioned a space where luxury beauty services weren't just appointments — they were experiences to be savoured.

Starting with a small, intimate studio in Beverly Hills, Glamore quickly gained a devoted clientele who appreciated the artistry, attention to detail, and the genuine care that defined every visit.

Today, Glamore is a full-service premium beauty destination with a team of 15+ certified specialists, thousands of happy clients, and a reputation built entirely on word-of-mouth excellence.

Our Journey

The Glamore Story

2018

Glamore Opens Its Doors

Our intimate 3-chair studio launches in Beverly Hills with Aria Fontaine at the helm and a small but passionate team.

2019

Nail Services Added

Zoe Nakamura joins as our first nail art specialist, bringing intricate artistry and our first dedicated nail suite.

2020

Best New Salon Award

Named Best New Beauty Studio by LA Style Magazine. An extraordinary honour during a challenging year.

2021

Studio Expansion

We triple our space, adding private suites, a dedicated bridal preparation room, and a luxury waiting lounge.

2022

Bridal Programme Launches

Celeste Rivera brings world-class bridal styling to Glamore, cementing our reputation as the go-to bridal beauty destination.

2023

1000+ Brides Served

A milestone moment — over 1,000 brides have walked out of Glamore feeling the most beautiful version of themselves.

2024

Luxury Day Packages

We launch our signature all-day studio experience — a full-day of pampering with a dedicated personal team.

2026

Continuing the Legacy

2,500+ clients, 15 specialists, and an unwavering commitment to making beauty an extraordinary experience.

Got Questions?

Pricing FAQs

Do you charge extra for consultations?
+

Never. All consultations at Glamore are completely complimentary. We believe that understanding your vision is the foundation of any great result, and we always want to get that right before we begin.

Why do prices vary from the starting rate?
+

Services like colour, extensions, and some treatments depend on factors like hair length, density, current condition, and the complexity of the look you want. We'll always give you a precise quote during your consultation before any work begins.

Is a deposit required to book?
+

Yes, we require a 25% deposit to secure your appointment. This is applied to your final service total. For bridal packages and full-day bookings, a 50% deposit is required. Deposits are fully refundable with 48 hours notice.

Do you offer group or bridal party discounts?
+

Absolutely. For bridal parties of 4 or more, we offer customised package pricing. Please contact us to discuss your specific group needs and we'll put together a bespoke quote.

What's included in the Studio Package price?
+

The Luxe Day package includes exclusive use of a private studio suite, a dedicated team of specialists, all services from our menu, gourmet refreshments, a luxury gift set, and access to our photo-ready studio area.
